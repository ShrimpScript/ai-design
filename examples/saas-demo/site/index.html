How an investigation works

It works the problem the way your best on-call engineer would, and shows its work.

Every step is a query you can rerun. Hypotheses it rules out stay in the report, so you can see what it checked and what it didn't.

Signals in

  • The alert and its history
  • Logs, metrics, traces
  • Deploys and config diffs
  • Your runbooks
→

Hypotheses ranked

  • Bad deploy #4812
  • payments-db saturation
  • Upstream card network
→

Evidence queries

  • logs: service:checkout-api status:5xx | top msg
  • deploys: since 02:40 | diff config
  • metrics: payments-db.cpu, .connections
→

Policy gate

Allowed at night: revert last deploy, if confidence is high and the diff touches config only.

Otherwise: page the on-call human with the report.

→

Outcome out

  • Root cause + confidence
  • Revert #4815, or a page
  • Slack summary + full report

Guardrails

You decide what it can touch. By default, nothing.

Reads

Always, with scoped read-only tokens

  • Logs, metrics, traces
  • Deploy history and diffs
  • Runbooks and past incidents

Proposes

A person approves in Slack

  • Reverts and rollbacks
  • Feature-flag changes
  • Scaling a deployment

Acts alone

Only what your policy file allows

  • Revert last deploy at night
  • Restart a crash-looping pod, once
  • Silence a known-noisy alert for 1h

Never

Not configurable

  • Database writes or deletes
  • IAM and secrets changes
  • Anything outside the policy

INC-2291 full report

checkout-api 5xx after deploy #4812

Summary

At 03:04 deploy #4812 changed db.pool.max_size from 50 to 5 for checkout-api. From 03:11 requests waited for a connection and timed out after 2s, and the 5xx rate reached 8.4%. Firstlight reverted the change at 03:16 under the night policy; errors returned to 0.2% by 03:21.

Evidence

03:12:40  logs     service:checkout-api status:5xx  → 2,341 lines
          top msg  "timeout acquiring connection from pool"  ×1,902
03:13:18  deploys  since 02:40 → #4812 (checkout-api, cart, tax, email)
          diff     config/prod.yaml  db.pool.max_size: 50 → 5
03:14:02  metrics  payments-db cpu 31%  conns 12/400  locks 0
03:14:48  health   cart ok · tax ok · email ok

Ruled out

  • payments-db saturation: CPU and connections normal, no lock waits
  • Card network outage: provider status green, no upstream 5xx
  • Traffic spike: requests/min within 4% of last Tuesday

Actions

  1. 03:16 Revert #4815 opened and deployed (policy: night-revert)
  2. 03:21 Recovery confirmed; on-call not paged
  3. Follow-up for the team: the pool change was meant for staging
