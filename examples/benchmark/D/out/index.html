      From your order to your bread bag

      1. Order by Thursday, 8pm

        We count every reservation on Thursday night and mix exactly that much dough, plus a few loaves for the counter.

      2. We bake on Friday night

        The dough proves overnight in the cold and goes into the oven from 3am, so it's still warm when the doors open.

      3. Collect on Saturday morning

        Give your name at the side hatch. Your bread is bagged and labelled; you pay by card or cash on the spot.

      A two-person bakery in a former laundrette

      Ines and Tom Okafor-Hale started baking for the street during the 2019 power cut, when the gas oven was the only one on the block still working. Six years on, it's the same deck oven, the same starter, and about 200 loaves every Saturday.

      We use stoneground flour from Holme Mill, forty miles north, and nothing that isn't flour, water, salt, seeds or butter. Pre-orders mean we throw almost nothing away; whatever is left at noon goes to the St Aidan's community fridge.

      Before you order

      What if I can't make it on Saturday?

      Cancel from the link in your confirmation email any time before Thursday 8pm. After that, send a friend with your name or reference. Bread not collected by noon goes to the community fridge.

      Can you slice my loaf?

      Yes. Tell us at the hatch and we'll slice it while you wait. We don't slice in advance because the crust softens in the bag.

      Do you bake anything gluten-free?

      No. Everything is made in one small room full of wheat and rye flour, so we can't promise anything is safe for coeliacs.

      Can I order for more than one Saturday?

      Not yet. Orders open each Saturday at noon for the following weekend, so the list above always shows the next bake.
